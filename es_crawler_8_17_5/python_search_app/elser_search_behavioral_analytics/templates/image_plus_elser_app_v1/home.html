🍳 Welcome to Elasticsearch based Recipe Search App

Looking for a recipe? Ingredients? Directions? Or something more specific? You're in the right place!

You can search for recipes using any keyword, term, or phrase. You can also search by image if you have one, or just enter the URL of the image.

Search by Text Search by Image

How it works:

Text Search

Simply enter any keyword, ingredient, or phrase you're looking for, and we'll find the recipes that match!

Image Search

Have an image or an image URL? Upload it or provide a URL, and we'll find recipes related to it.

Flexible Results

Whether it's ingredients, directions, or recipe titles, we’ll display results tailored to your search.
